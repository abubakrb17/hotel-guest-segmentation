# Images

This folder contains screenshots and visual outputs from the Hotel Guest Segmentation project.

## Included visuals

Project visuals may include:

- Elbow Method for Optimal Number of Clusters
- Silhouette Score by Number of Clusters
- Customer Distribution by Guest Segment
- Share of Customers by Guest Segment
- Average Revenue by Guest Segment
- Guest Segment Behavioral Profiles

These visuals support the interpretation of the clustering results and help explain the business meaning of each guest segment.
